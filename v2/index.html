Featured Graduation Research & Thesis

Simulation and Security Analysis of Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) Protocols

University of Babylon — Cybersecurity Dept. Author: Ali Nasser 2024–2025

This research delivers an in-depth computational modeling and security verification of Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) implementations, focusing on the BB84 and decoy-state paradigms. It analyzes the mathematical security boundaries against eavesdropping vectors (Intercept-Resend, Photon-Number-Splitting attacks) and explores quantum bit error rate (QBER) thresholds required for secure key distillation in post-quantum computational environments.
